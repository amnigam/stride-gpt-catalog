## 1. Background

Procurement wants a portal so suppliers can register themselves instead of e-mailing forms. Suppliers will enter their details and upload some documents, and the procurement team will approve them.

## 2. Solution

The portal will be built by the procurement technology team and hosted in the cloud. It will follow the Corvane standards for security. Architecture to be confirmed with the Cloud Platform Team.
